Biography

My name is Sara Salama, and I am a senior at Hunter College majoring in Media Studies. I have always been interested in media, especially social media, content creation, and digital design. I enjoy learning about the creative side of media as well as how it is used to communicate with different audiences. I am taking Web Production I because I want to learn more about how websites are created and develop skills that I can use in my future career.

I also enjoy photography, traveling, fashion, and spending time with my family and friends. After graduating, I hope to continue working in the media industry and eventually build a career that combines my interests in creative direction, content, and digital media.
